About

Our name means weaving.

BUNON comes from বুনন, the Bangla word for weaving — the slow, patient work of crossing thread over thread until something strong and beautiful takes shape.

We work with artisans and natural materials to make a small collection of everyday pieces. No seasons, no excess — just things made well, meant to be used and kept.

  • 01

    Natural materials

    Cotton, jute, linen and seagrass — chosen for how they feel, wear and age.

  • 02

    Made by hand

    Every piece is woven or stitched by skilled artisans, so no two are exactly alike.

  • 03

    Built to last

    Fewer, better things — designed to be used daily, repaired and passed on.
